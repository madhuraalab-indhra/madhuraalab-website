Non-Communicable Diseases (NCD) Protocol

தொற்றா நோய்கள் (NCD) விழிப்புணர்வு & பரிசோதனைகள்

சர்க்கரை நோய், உயர் இரத்த அழுத்தம், தைராய்டு, இதயம் மற்றும் சிறுநீரக பாதிப்புகளை ஆரம்பத்திலேயே கண்டறிய கிளினிக்கல் வழிகாட்டுதல்கள்.

1. Diabetes Mellitus Management

Glycemic Control & Organ Protection

A single fasting sugar test is not enough. Routine monitoring of HbA1c (3-month average sugar) along with Microalbuminuria is essential to protect kidneys and eyes from diabetic damage.

Recommended Routine Screening:
• Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) & Post Prandial (PPBS)
• HbA1c (3 Months Average)
• Urine Microalbumin / Creatinine Ratio

2. Hypertension & Lipid Care

Cardiovascular Risk Assessment

High Blood Pressure combined with elevated LDL Cholesterol significantly increases arterial blockage risks. Complete Lipid Profile measures Total Cholesterol, HDL, LDL, VLDL & Triglycerides.

Recommended Routine Screening:
• Lipid Profile (9 Parameters)
• Doorstep ECG Recording
• Serum Electrolytes (Sodium, Potassium, Chloride)

3. Fatty Liver & Hepatic Health

LFT & Enzyme Monitoring

Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD) is rising rapidly. Liver Function Test (LFT) checks SGOT, SGPT, Bilirubin, Alkaline Phosphatase and Albumin levels.

4. Thyroid Disorder & Fatigue

TSH & Metabolic Regulation

Unexplained weight changes, tiredness, or hair fall can stem from Hypothyroidism or Hyperthyroidism. TSH / Total Thyroid Profile helps evaluate gland activity.
